ICT Department Handbook
=======================
Assessment weighting   Continuous assessment 40%, final examination 60%.
Submission             Through the departmental portal, before 17:00 on the due date.
Late work              Capped at the pass mark unless supported by documentation.
Lab access             Monday to Friday, 08:00-18:00. Card access outside these hours.
Equipment              Report faults to the technician on duty, not to the lecturer.
